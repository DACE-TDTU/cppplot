## Priority 3: Level 3–4 Exercises (14 chapters missing)

**Problem identification exercises** test Pillar 3 — the highest-value skill. Each chapter needs ≥2 exercises that ask:
- "What is the *real* problem?" (not just "calculate X")
- "Is this the right formulation?"
- "Given these symptoms, diagnose the cause"

### Exercises to Add (2 per chapter):

| Chapter | Exercise A (Problem Identification) | Exercise B (Challenge Assumptions) |
|---------|------------------------------------|------------------------------------|
| **Ch 1** | "A home thermostat oscillates ±2°C. Is the problem the controller, the sensor delay, or the actuator? How would you diagnose each?" | "Explain using signal-flow arguments HOW the on-off thermostat compensates for outside temperature drops without ever measuring outside temperature." |
| **Ch 2** | "Two engineers propose different models for a DC motor: one 1st-order, one 3rd-order. When is each appropriate? What physical effects distinguish them?" | "Your motor model predicts 5% overshoot; the real motor shows 25%. List three physical effects the model might be missing, ranked by likelihood." |
| **Ch 3** | "An engineer models a boost converter as minimum-phase and designs a high-bandwidth controller. What will happen? Is the model formulation appropriate?" | "Two systems have identical Bode plots but different step responses. How is this possible? What does the transfer function hide?" |
| **Ch 4** | "A pharmaceutical reactor overshoots by 30°C on startup. Is the problem the controller gain, the model order, or the sensor delay? How would you determine which?" | "A colleague argues that critical damping (ζ=1) is always optimal. Construct a scenario where ζ=0.5 is a better design choice." |
| **Ch 5** | "A position servo oscillates at low gain. The root locus predicts stability for all K>0. List 3 physical effects not captured by G(s)=1/[s(s+a)] that could explain this." | "A colleague proposes doubling the gear ratio. Use root locus reasoning to argue whether this helps or hurts." |
| **Ch 6** | "A power supply oscillates at 2 kHz. The engineer suspects insufficient phase margin. Could the problem be something else? List three alternatives and explain how Bode analysis would distinguish them." | "An audio amplifier is designed for flat response to 20 kHz but sounds 'muddy'. The Bode plot shows −3dB at 18 kHz. Is the Bode plot telling the whole story?" |
| **Ch 7** | "A chemical reactor has time delay varying 0.5–2s. The standard approach designs for nominal 1s delay. What could go wrong? Propose an alternative problem formulation." | "Bode analysis shows PM=50° (comfortable). Nyquist analysis reveals conditional stability. Who is right? What is the real problem?" |
| **Ch 8** | "Your lag compensator meets PM=45° on the nominal plant, but the plant gain varies ±50%. Is this design robust? Reframe as a robust stability question." | "A colleague designs separate lead compensators for a 2×2 MIMO system by treating it as two SISO loops. What could go wrong?" |
| **Ch 9** | "A 6-DOF robot arm has 12 states but only 6 encoders. Is the state-space formulation usable? What is the real problem?" | "Your colleague models a DC motor as 2nd-order. You observe oscillations the model doesn't predict. What state is missing?" |
| **Ch 10** | "A factory has 3 sensors but 5 states. Which sensors should be placed where to ensure observability? Is full observability even necessary?" | "The controllability matrix is technically full-rank but nearly singular. Is the system practically controllable? What does the condition number tell you?" |
| **Ch 11** | "A colleague proposes placing all poles at s=−100 for 'maximum speed'. Explain why this is catastrophic and identify the real constraint." | "Your quadcopter has a broken motor sensor. Can you still achieve adequate control? Reformulate the problem." |
| **Ch 12** | "Observer + controller works in sim but oscillates on hardware. Model friction is 50% wrong. Is the problem the observer, the controller, or the model?" | "When should you use an observer vs. adding a physical sensor? Analyze cost, reliability, and dynamic performance." |
| **Ch 13** | "A deadbeat controller works in simulation but oscillates on hardware. List three possible causes ranked by likelihood." | "Your embedded controller has 512 bytes of RAM. The bilinear (Tustin) transform requires more memory than forward Euler. When is the trade-off worth it?" |
| **Ch 14** | "LQR makes a flexible robot arm vibrate at modes NOT in the model. Diagnose the problem and propose a fix." | "Compare LQR and pole placement for an inverted pendulum. Under what conditions does each fail? Which would you ship in a product?" |
